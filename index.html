Casto support

Casto helps you identify trading cards and keep a collection on your phone. It is free for collectors aged 13 and older.

How do I add my first card?

Choose a companion, open Scan, and point the camera at a card. You can also search by name. Check the set and number before adding it to your case.

Can I move my collection?

In Settings, select Save a backup file. Keep a copy somewhere you control. Use Bring in a backup file on the other phone. Uninstalling the app can erase your local collection.

Casto privacy policy

Version 1.0 · Updated November 23, 2026

Casto stores your card collection, trainer name, companion, notes and optional voice recordings on your phone. Rigpea Wangchuk publishes Casto. Casto is free and intended for collectors aged 13 and older. You can use it without an account. Casto has no ads or cross-app tracking.

Camera, photos and microphone

Scanning reads a card on your device using Google ML Kit. Casto writes a frame to the temporary app cache for recognition. Picking a photo works the same way. Card images from your camera or photo library and raw OCR results are not uploaded to Google. The card name or catalog ID is used for the catalog lookup below. Casto uses the microphone only when you choose to record a companion voice.

ML Kit sends Google technical usage and diagnostic data, including device and app information, an installation identifier, recognition events, performance and errors. Google describes this for iOS and Android.

Card catalog

In iOS build 2, the English TCGdex catalog is included in the app. Searching, scanning and checking a card use that copy, even on your first use in airplane mode. Card names, catalog IDs, searches and IP addresses are never sent to TCGdex by this build. Catalog updates arrive with app updates. Casto draws the card fronts on your phone.

Earlier TestFlight build 1 uses live catalog lookups. It sends card names or IDs and set-list requests to TCGdex, whose servers receive the connecting IP address. These requests exclude trainer names, notes, recordings and collections.

Backups and deletion

Settings lets you export and import a collection backup. A file you save or share can leave the device, and your phone’s system backup may include app data. Casto has no server copy from which to restore your collection.

Reset Casto clears your collection and profile. Delete recorded voices separately or remove the app. You control any exported or system backups. On Android, an optional widget and wallpaper display images locally on the phone.

Support and privacy questions

Email rigpea@gakhill.com. If something goes wrong, include your phone model, Casto version and the screen you were on. A copy of your collection is unnecessary.
